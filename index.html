Where are Kerala’s working women?

The recent PLFS data on Female Labour Force Participation rates throws up some surprises1. Kerala low in FLFP (41.2%) & Delhi the lowest (13.1%)?! Anecdotal evidence would suggest otherwise for both these places. So what is going on?

I have been studying Kerala's changing livelihoods as a project through PLFS data, and the pandemic seems to have pushed both rural and urban women in agriculture, largely from construction, education, finance & health-social work. This can be seen in the chart above as well, a steep rise in Kerala FLFPR from 2020-21 to 2021-22 which has since sustained.

The two series cover different reference periods. July–June years are PLFS agricultural years and calendar years run January–December, so the lines aren't joined across the divider. Over 2022–2024 the two series overlap in time.

2025 isn't directly comparable with earlier years. PLFS switched to a new sample design in January 2025, and MoSPI cautions against comparing results from then on with earlier ones. The 2024→2025 change (dashed) mixes any real change with the effect of the redesign.
