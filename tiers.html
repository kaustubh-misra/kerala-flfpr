A quirk of PLFS: Agrarian economies show high FLFPRs

Possible reason: agriculture counts as employment in the PLFS, and that single measurement choice fundamentally shapes these rankings.

In MoSPI's PLFS, Activity Status Code 21 defines an individual who worked as a helper in a household enterprise (unpaid family worker). This means agrarian states rank higher by default (Nagaland, MP, Rajasthan etc.), even if women are largely engaged in subsistence & unpaid employment.

Kerala's economy is driven by services: agricultural output is 9.30% of the total economic output.

Sources

Female LFPR: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS), Annual Report July 2023 – June 2024. Female, age 15+, rural + urban, usual status (ps+ss).

GSVA: MoSPI, State-wise Gross State Value Added by economic activity, 2011-12 base series, current prices, 2023-24. State estimates are compiled by the State/UT Directorates of Economics and Statistics. Recent years may be provisional and subject to revision.

Both retrieved from the MoSPI eSankhyiki API (PLFS indicator 1; NAS indicator 29, base year 2011-12) on 26 October 2026.

Agri GSVA share, tiers and ranks are our own calculation: GSVA from “Agriculture, Livestock, Forestry and Fishing” ÷ “Total Gross State Value Added”. Tiers: High above 25%, Mid 15–25%, Low below 15%. Dadra & Nagar Haveli & Daman & Diu and Lakshadweep have no GSVA data and are not classified.

Each line is the simple (unweighted) average of the states and UTs in that tier, so Sikkim counts as much as Uttar Pradesh. Averages are rounded to one decimal. The hover readout shows how many states each average covers. Ladakh has no data before 2019-20, and Chandigarh has no rural value for 2023-24, 2024 or 2025.

Tiers are fixed using each state's 2023-24 agriculture share of GSVA at current prices: High above 25%, Mid 15–25%, Low below 15%. States don't change tier between years. Dadra & Nagar Haveli & Daman & Diu and Lakshadweep have no GSVA data and are left out.

July–June years and calendar years cover different periods, so the lines aren't joined across the divider. 2025 uses PLFS's new sample design and isn't directly comparable with earlier years, so the 2024→2025 segment is drawn faded.

Source: MoSPI eSankhyiki API (PLFS indicator 1; NAS indicator 29). State-level data (JSON)
